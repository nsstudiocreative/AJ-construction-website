About us

Commitment to craftsmanship

AJ Construction Inc. is a locally owned construction company headquartered in Hudson, Massachusetts. We serve homeowners, property managers and commercial clients throughout the MetroWest region and New England, managing every project with the same attention to detail and care we would bring to our own home. A single, dedicated team oversees each project from foundation to final finish, ensuring one coordinated schedule and one point of contact throughout.
